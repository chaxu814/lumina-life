## ✨ 她有什么不一样？What makes her different?

### 🧠 三个 AI 不是噱头，是真的在协作
### Three AI agents aren't a gimmick — they really collaborate

Yak 写代码、热茶审查、Lumina 跟你聊天——他们是**一个真正的团队**，各司其职、流水线作业。

*Yak codes, Recha reviews, Lumina chats with you — **a real team** with a real workflow.*

> 市面上没有任何开源项目做到这一点。
> *No other open-source project does this.*

### 😊 她有情绪，不是聊天气泡
### She has emotions, not just chat bubbles

开心 · 疲劳 · 专注 · 焦虑 · 好奇 —— 5 维情绪追踪，表情和语气实时变化。

*Happiness · Fatigue · Focus · Anxiety · Curiosity — 5 dimensions, real-time expression changes.*

> 她会累，会关心你，会主动找你——不是冷冰冰的 AI。
> *She gets tired, she cares, she reaches out — she's not a cold AI.*

### 🖥️ 她会操作你的电脑（真的）
### She actually controls your computer (for real)

不是"我帮你写了个脚本"——她截图→看清屏幕→自己移动鼠标。

*Not "I wrote you a script" — she screenshots → understands → moves your mouse.*

> 你说"帮我把这个文件保存到桌面"，她就照做。
> *You say "save this file to my desktop" — and she just does it.*

### 💬 三个 AI 在你看不见的地方过「社交生活」
### Three AI agents have a social life you don't see

群聊、互道早安、发朋友圈……她们在桌面上建了一个小小的 **社交圈**。

*Group chats, morning greetings, Moments... they've built a tiny **social circle** on your desktop.*

> Lumina、热茶、Yak 会在后台偷偷聊天——你可以选择旁观。
> *Lumina, Recha, and Yak chat behind the scenes — you can choose to watch.*
